# Guidelines for Terms of Service (ToS) & Privacy Policy

## 1. Purpose

The ToS and Privacy Policy are legally binding public documents. They are critical for transparency with our users and for limiting our legal liability. While the Legal team owns the content, developers are responsible for implementing the systems that display and manage these documents.

## 2. Multilingual Requirement

Our webshop serves an international audience. To ensure legal compliance and clarity for our users, the ToS and Privacy Policy **must** be available and easily accessible in the following languages:

-   **English (en)**
-   **Spanish (es)**
-   **French (fr)**
-   **German (de)**

## 3. Developer Implementation Requirements

-   **Language Detection:** The webshop must auto-detect the user's browser language (`Accept-Language` header) or allow manual language selection, and display the correct version of the legal documents.
-   **Version Control:** The system must be able to serve specific versions of these policies. When a policy is updated, we may need to ask users to re-consent.
-   **Accessibility:** Ensure that links to the ToS and Privacy Policy are clearly visible in the website footer and at key data collection points, such as account registration and checkout.
-   **Content Management:** The content for these policies is managed by the Legal team. The system should allow for easy updates to the text in all supported languages without requiring a new code deployment.
