# Internal Compliance & Privacy Policy

## 1. Mission Statement

Our company is committed to the highest standards of data protection and privacy. We build trust with our customers by handling their data transparently and securely. Our goal is to embed privacy and security into the core of our products and processes.

## 2. Core Principles

All software development must adhere to the following principles:

-   **Privacy by Design and by Default:** Privacy is not an add-on; it is a fundamental component of our system architecture. We build systems to be privacy-preserving from the start, with the strictest privacy settings applied by default.
-   **Data Minimization:** We only collect, process, and store data that is absolutely necessary for a legitimate and specified purpose.
-   **Purpose Limitation:** Data collected for one purpose will not be used for another incompatible purpose without explicit consent or legal basis.
-   **Security by Design:** Security is a primary consideration throughout the development lifecycle, from requirements gathering to deployment and maintenance.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

-   **Data Protection Officer (DPO):** The DPO is the primary contact for all data protection matters. They oversee compliance and provide guidance. Contact: `dpo@our-company.com`.
-   **Security Champions:** Designated developers within each team who act as the first line of defense for security. They promote best practices and assist in security reviews.
-   **All Developers:** Every developer is responsible for understanding and implementing the policies and guidelines outlined in this documentation. When in doubt, ask your team's Security Champion or the DPO.
